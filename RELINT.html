Our Mission

To manage Foreign Trade, Foreign Investment, Economic Collaboration, and International Relations activities in Havana, raising efficiency and developing potential across these areas while achieving maximum participation from municipalities and entities.

General Functions

  1. Execute and control compliance with state and government-approved policies in international relations, foreign trade, foreign investment, and economic collaboration.
  2. Guide and coordinate the identification and promotion of exportable goods and services, import substitution, and foreign investment opportunities in local development.
  3. Guide and coordinate the identification and promotion of foreign investment opportunities in local development.
  4. Coordinate and control the development of economic collaboration provided by the province together with management bodies and other territorial entities.
  5. Coordinate and control economic collaboration received from abroad and its incorporation into the Economic Plan.
  6. Coordinate and control the attention to foreign scholars studying or residing in the territory.
  7. Manage and ensure the international relations of the Provincial Administration.
